# PacketPulse 2.0 — Decision Register & Governance

## Decision handling

A decision entry should preserve its ID, date/source, decision text, rationale, alternatives, scope, consequences, status and related requirements/design/tasks. IDs are permanent; never recycle them.

## Recorded governance

- DEC-035 / DEC-036 / DEC-037 have historical cross-document reuse. Preserve distinct source contexts as history keys; do not silently reinterpret.
- DEC-048–053 relate to the 25 Nov review/discussion set; descriptions for 050–053 are not fully recovered in the available source and remain gaps.
- DEC-054–061 appear in the 26 Nov Product Guide and concern expanded prototype/design scope including media, embeds, nested UX and pattern/creative direction. Refer to the canonical source for exact individual wording.

## Approved Prototype definition

Approved Prototype = include in the next review build. It is not production, legal, security or release approval, and does not prove implementation. Team review may retain, refine, restrict or remove it.

## Safety boundaries

Automated skill-verification work must be controlled, evidence-based, policy-versioned, auditable, appealable and reversible. Do not infer permission to automate hiring selection, money movement or sensitive account actions.
