# PacketPulse Community

Status: In progress

## Purpose

Run PacketPulse as a practical networking community that helps learners and working professionals build skills, find useful resources, and connect with relevant opportunities.

## Community context

- PacketPulse was previously referred to as Tshoot / PacketPulse.
- The community had 1,740+ members as of 8 September 2026.
- The audience includes CCNA/CCNP learners and experienced professionals interested in networking, NOC/support roles, firewalls, SD-WAN, cloud networking, security, and automation.
- The community's intended role is a bridge between candidates, practical learning, and recruiters. The goal is to surface relevant opportunities for members; outreach should not imply that the admin is personally seeking a job.
- Community website recorded: [https://packetpulse.vercel.app](https://packetpulse.vercel.app)
- LinkedIn presence recorded as PacketPulse.
- Contact email recorded: [packetpulse@outlook.com](mailto:packetpulse@outlook.com)

## Learning and content initiatives

- Practical CCNA lab learning, including VLAN and inter-VLAN routing topics.
- Palo Alto and Panorama lab initiative discussed for weekday 8:30 PM sessions, using a rented server and firewall licences. The recorded cost context was approximately ₹2,000 per month for server rent and ₹2,000 one-time for licences; confirm current availability and costs before restarting or promoting it.
- Educational explainers and visual learning material for networking fundamentals, including DHCP, DNS, MAC address resolution, and packet delivery.
- Documentation and practical guides across Cisco IOS/IOS-XE/NX-OS, PAN-OS, Linux, FortiGate, SD-WAN, and packet analysis tools such as tcpdump.

## Product and community direction

Ideas previously discussed for a future PacketPulse 2.0:

- Skill assessments and a skill-verified badge for members who pass them.
- A structured library of labs, courses, sessions, notes, and learning resources.
- Dedicated career and recruiter workflows to connect relevant openings with members.
- Community nodes / connections visual language for the website and brand.
- A possible finance or personal-planning module was discussed as an idea, not a committed feature.

## Voice and positioning

- Keep communication human, professional, concise, and genuine.
- Avoid sales-heavy language, exaggerated promises, or money-making claims.
- Use a premium editorial and technical-minimal visual direction.
- Prioritise practical value, trust, learning, and useful connections.
