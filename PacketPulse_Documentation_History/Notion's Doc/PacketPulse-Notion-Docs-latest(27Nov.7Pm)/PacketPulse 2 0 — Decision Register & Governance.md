## Approved Prototype definition

Approved Prototype = include in the next review build. It is not production, legal, security or release approval, and does not prove implementation. Team review may retain, refine, restrict or remove it.

## Safety boundaries

Automated skill-verification work must be controlled, evidence-based, policy-versioned, auditable, appealable and reversible. Do not infer permission to automate hiring selection, money movement or sensitive account actions.

## Design governance note — user psychology before aesthetics (27 Nov 2026)

Owner-confirmed direction: PacketPulse must be designed with explicit thought for user psychology and lived context. This is a guiding design constraint for future artifacts and screen specs, not approval of any particular visual theme or implementation.

Required review questions: Who is this for and in what context? What task/uncertainty does it address? What does the hierarchy ask the user to notice? Does it preserve autonomy and dignity? Is trust/status truthful and explainable? Is motion useful, accessible and performant? What evidence will determine retain/refine/restrict/remove?

Avoid trend-led generic AI/fintech styling, decorative HUDs, ambient glow/3D without user value, engagement manipulation, false urgency and invented status. Creative exploration remains open, but product meaning and usability must lead.
