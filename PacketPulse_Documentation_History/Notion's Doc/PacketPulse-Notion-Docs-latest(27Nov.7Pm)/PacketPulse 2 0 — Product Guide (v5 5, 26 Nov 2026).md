# Product Guide — Vision, Requirements and Decisions

**Source version:** 5.5 · **Source date:** 26 November 2026

## Purpose and product identity

PacketPulse 2.0 is an engineering community and career platform connecting learning, hands-on practice, skill evidence, professional identity and opportunity. Tagline: **Connect, Learn & Grow Together**. The product loop is Learn → Practice → Prove → Verify → Connect → Get hired → Grow.

## Core value

- Engineers: evidence-backed professional capability rather than unverifiable resume claims.
- Recruiters: relevant candidates with visible, reviewable evidence.
- Community: useful technical knowledge and contribution reputation.
- Operations: governed review, moderation and audit trails.

## Product principles

1. Features support the capability-growth loop.
2. Evidence precedes verified labels.
3. Automation is governed, explainable, policy-versioned, appealable and reversible.
4. Trust, privacy and consent are designed into flows.
5. Shared account, skills, permissions, tokens and reusable components.
6. Accessible, spacious, emotionally considerate UX; avoid manipulative engagement.
7. Reward helpful knowledge contribution, not raw activity.

## Community as knowledge network

Community includes technical Q&A, production incidents, expert discussion, case studies, articles, groups, events and mentoring. Taxonomy connects contributions to courses, practice scenarios, assessments and skills. Community reputation remains distinct from verified skills, certifications and employment evidence.

## Scope and governance notes

- Existing stack is treated as fixed in this revision; no stack replacement is authorised by this document.
- Approved Prototype means include for review, not production/legal/security approval or proof of implementation.
- Automated skill-verification modes may be tested under controlled policy and feature flags; hiring, financial and sensitive account decisions remain separately governed and human-confirmed absent explicit approval.
- The guide records requirements, IDs, decision references, risk register and change history. The production repository audit is separate and is required before claiming implementation.

## Design principle update — 27 Nov 2026

PacketPulse design decisions must begin with the user's context, task, psychological friction, trust needs and autonomy—not a visual trend. Make clarity, evidence, dignity, belonging and meaningful progress part of the product experience. Avoid manipulative retention, false urgency, arbitrary scores and misleading status. Creative artifacts and micro-animations must have a clear user purpose, accessible states, reduced-motion behavior and validation criteria. This is an owner-confirmed direction; it does not approve a new theme or claim implementation.
