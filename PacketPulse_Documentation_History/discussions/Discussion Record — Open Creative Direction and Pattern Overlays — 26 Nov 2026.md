# PacketPulse 2.0 — Discussion Record: Open Creative Direction and Pattern Overlays

**Date:** 26 November 2026  
**Scope:** Brand identity, design-system flexibility, reusable visual motifs and pattern assets  
**Status:** Owner-confirmed direction; included in Approved Prototype review scope  

## 1. Owner direction

The design system must not become a rigid visual rulebook. The owner wants the team to be creative, experimental and proactive about improving the complete product experience. Existing examples, recommended libraries, named pattern families and sample colours are starting points. They do not define the full boundary of what PacketPulse may explore.

## 2. Colour direction

- Use the complete Signal Grove brand, module and semantic token system.
- Do not make every motif or component green. Forest/eucalyptus/lime are useful brand colours, but indigo, blue, plum, violet, cyan, azure, amber, coral, red, neutrals, tonal ramps and new combinations may be explored.
- Experiment with single-colour linework, two-tone routes, multi-hue waves, gradient nodes, spectrum treatments and other expressive but legible colour stories.
- Pantone references may inform brand and print exploration. Record an official Pantone guide/edition and coated/uncoated reference only when verified; do not invent PMS numbers or infer a precise Pantone match from HEX alone.
- Record the actual digital colour values and test their use in light, dark and Matte Black contexts.

## 3. Pattern-only scope

The owner explicitly clarified: **do not change the existing background** for the current pattern exercise. Design patterns as reusable, optional, transparent overlays that can be applied to existing surfaces. The pattern can be omitted without changing the component.

Potential placements include card corners, card headers, profile covers, featured-resource panels, onboarding accents, empty states, section dividers, media cards, page headers and branded moments. The same motif should adapt through crop, scale, density, stroke weight, colour and opacity.

## 4. Pattern vocabulary (expandable)

- Packet routes and branching paths.
- Node constellations and network meshes.
- Signal waves and overlapping wave bands.
- Travelling pulses and pulse trails.
- Radar rings, arcs and detection points.
- Secure layers, shield contours and hexagonal geometry.
- Data-flow ribbons, particles and dotted fields.
- Packet matrices, grids and modular cells.
- Any new motif that strengthens recognition or communicates a useful idea.

Waves and pulses are explicitly welcome. Do not limit future exploration to these eight families.

## 5. Motion direction

Patterns may have static and animated variants. Examples include packets travelling along paths, nodes lighting up in sequence, a pulse radiating from a node, a wave passing through a card edge or a radar arc sweeping slowly. Motion can be expressive; it should be adapted to context and never obscure content or controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences and provide an equivalent static presentation.

## 6. What remains important

Creative freedom does not remove product-quality expectations. Evaluate new directions for readability, accessibility, responsive behaviour, usability, performance, privacy, security and licensing. These are review criteria, not a reason to avoid experimentation.

## 7. Review-build treatment

All pattern and colour explorations discussed here are **Approved Prototype** for inclusion in the review build as variants or sample components. Team feedback can result in Retain, Refine, Restrict or Remove in the next build. This is not production approval.

## 8. Documentation destinations

- Document 1: record owner design-exploration and pattern-scope decisions.
- Document 2: preserve creative flexibility and distinguish experience guardrails from aesthetic preferences.
- Document 3: identify useful surfaces/workflows where overlays may be applied without changing backgrounds.
- Document 4: include transparent pattern assets in the build and visual-review tasks.
- Document 5: maintain an expandable pattern/colourway registry and source asset manifest.
